## Description
What does a knight sound like? Or a queen? Not a sound effect: the real sound, computed from the rules of chess and nothing else. Each piece becomes an instrument, its vibration modes become notes, and famous games turn into melodies.

In 1966, Mark Kac asked "Can one hear the shape of a drum?" Today: can you hear the shape of a chess piece?
